# Competitor & sector reference (inspiration only)

**Disclaimer:** This document summarizes **common public-web patterns** observed across the oilfield equipment and solids-control sector. It is **not** an endorsement, partnership claim, or instruction to copy assets, copy, or layouts. **Do not** reproduce proprietary text, photography, datasheets, or trademarked layouts.

## Reference sources consulted (high level)

- **Large OEM / diversified energy equipment:** global manufacturers often use a **dark header**, **product-first navigation**, and deep **document libraries** (e.g. NOV-style “Products & Services” hubs with technical literature). Pattern: credibility through **taxonomy + downloads + standards**, not marketing fluff.  
- **Solids control specialists (examples encountered in search):** sites such as **KOSUN**, **GN Solids Control**, **Aipu Solid Control**, **TR Solids Control (example from sector)** tend to feature:  
  - **Category tiles** for shale shaker, centrifuge, agitator, tanks, etc.  
  - **Application** tags (onshore/offshore/HDD) — use only if true for Hongyu.  
  - **Certification badges** where genuinely held — Hongyu materials cite **ISO 9001 / 45001 / 14001** only.  
  - **Prominent RFQ / contact** in header and end-of-page **CTA band**.

## Recurring UX patterns (non-proprietary)

1. **Hero:** industrial background, one-line category (“Solids control equipment manufacturer”), dual CTA (Products / Contact).  
2. **Product catalog:** 2–3 levels max at first launch: **category → product family → spec table**.  
3. **Specs:** HTML tables + PDF datasheet links (we avoid linking internal `ref/` PDFs publicly in v1).  
4. **Trust:** certification **names**, factory stats **if verified**, **patent** or innovation section when factual.  
5. **Sticky bar** on mobile with **Call** or **Email**—use **hotline** we have; email TODO.  
6. **Footer:** address, compliance, sitemap, privacy placeholder.

## What we intentionally avoid

- Stock “startup” gradients and cartoon illustrations.  
- Invented **API monogram**, **country counts**, or ** rig counts**.  
- Cloned menu labels or marketing slogans from other brands.

## Takeaways for Hongyu prototype

- Mirror **structure** (Products / About / Contact, spec-forward cards) without mimicking any single competitor skin.  
- Keep **claims** aligned with `docs/content-brief.md`.
