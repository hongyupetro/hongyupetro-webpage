## Specific product: TCNJ series mud agitator (from 宏宇搅拌器说明书中英文-2024)

**Role:** Part of the **drilling-fluid solids control system**; mounted on the mud tank with impeller submerged; agitates fluid to mix additives/clays, help disperse solids, reduce settling, and help maintain stable fluid properties for safe drilling.

**Major assemblies (CN):** explosion-proof motor, reducer, base, guard, coupling, impeller shaft, impeller (防爆电机、减速器、底座、护罩、联轴器、叶轮轴、叶轮).

**Design notes (from Chinese section):** horizontal motor installation; **single-stage arc cylindrical worm gear reducer**; **imported oil seals** on input/output shaft ends for sealing reliability and life; low center of gravity, stable operation, mixing performance, appearance.

- **Impeller diameter:** upper **920 mm**, lower **430 mm** (table text).  
- **Blade angle:** **45°**.  
- **Rated power options** (table text, fragmented): **4, 5.5, 7.5, 11, 15, 18.5, 22 kW** (confirm exact model-to-power mapping from original PDF tables).
